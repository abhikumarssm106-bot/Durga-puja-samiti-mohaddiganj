Sacred Ritual & Sisterhood
calendar_today Vijayadashami schedule 10:30 AM

Sindoor Khela & Baran Utsav

location_on Garbha Griha & Pandal Mandap

Sacred farewell ritual celebrating sisterhood and traditional greeting. Devotee mothers and daughters offer sweets, betel leaves, and vermilion to Maa Durga before bidai.

Ritual Details

All women devotees welcome. Pure natural vermilion and Sandesh offered by Samiti Seva Dal.

Civic Procession
calendar_today Vijayadashami schedule 04:00 PM

Visarjan Shobhayatra

location_on Mohaddiganj to River Ghat

Festive civic procession through Sasaram city to the ghats with royal brass bands, traditional conch blowing, and rhythmic Dhak ensembles bidding farewell to Maa.

Route & Safety

Coordinated with Rohtas district administration, volunteer guides stationed along entire heritage avenue.

Attending Cultural Programs

Mohaddiganj Durga Puja Samiti welcomes devotees and music connoisseurs from all across Bihar and Eastern UP. Please keep these recommendations in mind:

Arrive 30 Minutes Prior

accessible
