Fifty Years of Heritage

A Century of Faith Across Sasaram Lanes

The Mohaddiganj Durga Puja Samiti traces its devotional genealogy to early neighborhood elder-gatherings in the heart of Rohtas district. What started under modest canvas marquees has blossomed into one of Bihar's foremost epicenters of Shakta philosophy and temple craft.
