Honoring Sacred Traditions with Reverence

Every shloka recited during the Sharadiya Mahotsav follows strict Shakta Vidhi preserved across generations by the Mohaddiganj Samiti. Devotees offering Pushpanjali are requested to maintain traditional sanctity, collect fresh vilva patra (bel patra) from the distribution counters, and proceed in quiet meditation.

108 Lotus & 108 Diya Maha Sandhi Puja (11:45 AM)

The supreme highlight of Sharadiya Durga Puja honoring Maa Chamunda vanquishing Chanda and Munda. 108 hand-picked nilkamal (blue lotus) offerings paired with 108 brass earthen deepaks, followed by Balidan sankalpa.

Mahashtami Pushpanjali

Emotional farewell ritual. Married women perform Mother Durga's aarti with betel leaves and sweets, applying vermilion (sindoor) to the Devi and each other.

Devotee Guidelines & Seva Protocol

To preserve the divine serenity and ensure comfort for every family visiting the Mohaddiganj Durga Puja pandal, we kindly request all visitors to observe these sacred guidelines.

Pushpanjali Offerings

Clean and consecrated hibiscus, bel patra, and chandan are arranged gratis by the Samiti. External plastic packaging is strictly prohibited in the inner Sanctum.

Bhog Prasad Seva

Maha Bhog distribution starts strictly after noon bhog aarti on Ashtami and Navami. Orderly seating in the Annakshetra pandal ensures that up to 3,000 devotees dine concurrently.
