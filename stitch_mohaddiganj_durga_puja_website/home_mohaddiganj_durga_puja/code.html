Our Festival

A Celebration of Faith, Tradition & Community

For decades, the historic neighborhood of Mohaddiganj in Sasaram, Bihar, becomes an epicenter of devotion every Sharadiya Navratri. Echoing with the sacred chime of conch shells, the resonant rhythm of dhak drums, and Vedic chants, our Sarvajanik festival unites families across Rohtas.

Rooted in deep Shakta traditions, our Samiti upholds meticulous scriptural rites from Bodhon to Dashami Visarjan. Each year, master artisans craft breathtaking architectural pandals and divine clay pratimas that celebrate Bihar’s cultural warmth, hospitality, and communal brotherhood.

Custodian of Tradition

About Mohaddiganj Durga Puja Samiti

Established over half a century ago by the visionary elders of Mohaddiganj, our Samiti serves as a non-profit cultural trust committed to perpetuating traditional Vedic worship, empowering local folk artisans, and nurturing inter-generational community unity in Sasaram.

Every Navratri, over 200 youth and senior volunteers manage crowd logistics, elderly darshan assistance, civic sanitation, and pristine Maha Bhog distribution for thousands of daily visitors.
